Setup

Install it

Drag LifePet.app into Applications. First time, right-click it and choose Open. A double-click won't work, because the build isn't notarised. If macOS claims the app is damaged, that's quarantine, not a bad download. Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LifePet.app once.

Connect Brightspace

In Brightspace open Calendar, then Settings, and tick Enable Calendar Feeds. Subscribe only appears after you do, which is where most people get stuck. Choose All Calendars and Tasks, copy the link, paste it in.

Add your classes

In Google Calendar settings, open your calendar, choose Integrate calendar, and copy the secret address in iCal format. Or sign in with Google to get the lot at once.

Turn on chat, if you want it

A free key from aistudio.google.com goes under Brain. Everything else works without one. You just can't ask it anything.
