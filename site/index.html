The creature is in the notch.

Gone the moment you need the screen

One shortcut tucks it into the notch, where it becomes a thin strip that still shows what is next. Press it again and the creature drops back onto the desktop.

Setup

Install it

Drag LifePet.app into Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open: the build is not notarised, so a double-click will be refused. If macOS says the app is damaged, that is quarantine rather than a bad download — run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LifePet.app once.

Connect Brightspace

In Brightspace open Calendar, then Settings, and tick Enable Calendar Feeds. Only then does Subscribe appear — this is where almost everyone gets stuck. Choose All Calendars and Tasks, copy the link, and paste it into LifePet.

Add your classes

In Google Calendar settings, open your calendar, choose Integrate calendar and copy the secret address in iCal format. Or sign in with Google if you would rather have every calendar at once.

Turn on chat, if you want it

A free key from aistudio.google.com goes under Brain. Everything else works without one; you simply cannot ask it questions.

Printed deadlines, since 1655

This is an almanac printed in Utrecht in 1655: every date of the year set in red and black, so that a person could see what was coming. LifePet is the same idea, except it already knows your courses and it comes to find you.

It is waiting.

Download it, paste one calendar link, and the rest of the semester arrives on its own.
