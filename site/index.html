Set up in four steps

  1. Install it. Drag Curet.app into Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open: the build is not notarised yet, so a double-click is refused. If macOS says the app is damaged, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Curet.app once.
  2. Connect Brightspace. In Brightspace open Calendar, then Settings, and tick Enable Calendar Feeds. Only then does Subscribe appear, and this is where almost everyone gets stuck. Choose All Calendars and Tasks, copy the link, and paste it into Curet.
  3. Add your classes. Press Sign in with Google, or paste your calendar’s secret address in iCal format.
  4. Say good morning. Curet wakes up, shows what is due, and takes it from there. Chat is optional and turns on in Settings.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Curet is free and open source.

Does it read my email?

No. It only sees the calendars you connect, and nothing else.

Where does my data live?

On your Mac, in a private file. Calendar requests go to Google and to your Brightspace link. If you turn on chat, your questions and the details it needs go to Google’s Gemini.

Can it change my calendar?

Only when you ask. After you sign in with Google and allow it, Curet can add an event you describe. It never edits or deletes the ones you already have.

Does it work without Brightspace?

Yes. Google Calendar or any iCal link works on its own, and Brightspace just adds your due dates.

Which Macs does it run on?

Recent macOS on Apple silicon. On an Intel Mac you can build it from the source in a few minutes.

Why does macOS warn me?

Curet is not notarised yet. Right-click it, choose Open, and confirm once.
